FY 2026–27 Corporate Tax Efficiency Checklist

Authored by Rajesh Nair, Managing Partner • Published May 2026 • 6 Min Read

Why Early Fiscal Year Structuring Matters

Corporate tax efficiency is rarely achieved in March. Most measurable reductions in effective tax rate and statutory dispute exposure originate during Q1—when capital expenditure timing, related-party pricing, and advance tax projections remain flexible.

Advisory Key Takeaway: Aligning quarterly books-to-GST-to-TDS reconciliations cuts year-end audit adjustments and protects working capital against blocked tax credits.

Core Statutory Checkpoints for Finance Leaders

First, validate all inter-company service agreements against arm's-length documentation standards. Second, review supplier GST compliance profiles monthly before finalizing input tax credit claims under GSTR-3B. Third, verify TDS deduction sections across technical retainers and SaaS subscriptions.
